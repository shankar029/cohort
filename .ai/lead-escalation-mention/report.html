Lead-first escalation, @mention wake, Frontend no-code fix — Final report

Repo: cohort · Branch: feat/lead-escalation-mention · Commit: 00296cb · 2026-09-15 · Design: design.html · Review: review.md

1. Outcome

Cohort no longer dead-ends on a human "NEEDS YOUR INPUT" prompt when a specialist can't produce code. The Team Lead now steps in first: it makes the missing reversible decision (tech stack, file layout, conventions), writes a concrete buildable spec into the task, posts it to the agent, and re-drives — one-shot per item, with the human prompt kept as the guaranteed last-resort backstop. Users can also summon the Lead by @mention (@lead / @team-lead / @<lead name>) to make it take over parked no-code blockers immediately. This directly resolves the reported Frontend "no code" stall: the live-run diagnosis (empty greenfield repo + no stack decision → the FE looped on self-directed clarifying questions) is now rescued because the Lead supplies the decision. Committed on feat/lead-escalation-mention; PR to open (§10). Headline caveat: cross-restart @mention take-over is intentionally not supported (§8), and the FE fix is a mechanism + evidence-backed diagnosis rather than an FE-persona prompt edit.

2. Delivered

  • leadResolveBlocker — Lead makes+persists an unblock decision (<!--lead-decision--> marker) and re-drives; one-shot via alreadyLeadAssisted (in-memory + persisted).

  • leadTakeOverPending + chat() @mention wiring; scoped strictly to a noCodeQuestions map (B1) so build/integration/epic-review escalations are never swept or answered with an invalid choice.

8. Assumptions still unconfirmed

  • Cross-restart @mention take-over is NOT supported: noCodeQuestions is in-memory (it is the B1 scoping guard); this matches the pre-existing in-memory answer-resolver behavior.
  • @mention matches @lead/@team-lead/@teamlead/@<lead displayName>; leading non-word boundary rejects emails/hosts.
  • Item-1 scope centers on the no-code blocker path (the reported case); the helper is adoptable by the other raiseQuestion sites next.
