Testing plan

  • I1 (deterministic self-test). The --fake run is the unit/integration proof for the scoring: the fake's structured-breakdown turn persists a design (via designThenEnrich) and enriches tasks with <!--design-acceptance-->, and the fake writes files — so every new outcome field is exercised and asserted by the scenario's acceptance[]. This proves the harness measures the right thing without spending a live run. Regression scope: the other scenarios' until/scoring must be unaffected (additive fields; unary until compatibility) — re-run node evals/run.mjs smoke --fake to confirm.
  • I2 (live behavior). One real run is the end-to-end proof. Evidence bundle: evals/reports/<run>.md + .json, the WS events.jsonl, the persisted design text, and the frontend deliverable list — copied into .ai/live-greenfield-smoke/evidence/. One pass/fail line per AC. If the run times out or the frontend stalls, that is captured honestly and the owning AC is marked NOT-VERIFIED/BLOCKED, not argued away.
