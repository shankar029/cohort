Design-first orchestration — Final report

Repo: cohort · Branch: feat/design-first-orchestration (stacked on PR #2 base feat/lead-escalation-mention) · 2026-09-15 · Design: design.html · Review: review.md

1. Outcome

Cohort's Team Lead now runs design-first. For a non-trivial epic the Architect (or the Lead when no architect is on the team) produces a short technical design + a per-stream task breakdown before the team builds; the design is persisted and its acceptance criteria enrich each task. Trivial/simple work skips the design step — the Lead splits directly. A three-level fallback (architect → Lead → deterministic template) plus a synchronous template floor means the board is never stranded. Every builder persona now decides under uncertainty on a greenfield repo instead of looping on clarifying questions (the reported Frontend "no code" stall), and the Architect owns the greenfield stack decision while honoring an existing repo's AGENTS.md/README/docs. Agents resolve questions in bounded group discussions settled by a decider (PM for product, else Lead) that writes the decision back — instead of blocking the user. Delivered as 3 reviewed increments; PR to open (§10).

Headline caveats: the persona/stack behavior (AC4/AC5) is proven at the prompt-invariant level — its live effect needs the real LLM adapter (the offline fake writes files regardless of persona text); and design taken as-proposed without human sign-off (unattended "go ahead").
